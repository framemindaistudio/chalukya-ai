For the district one screen to run the day from

Live command centre during the morning of 25 September: expected visitors, people on site now, free parking, open alerts and feels-like heat.

What it does for the department

  • Crowd against capacity at six sites, hour by hour, with today's peak.
  • SOS and heat alerts from visitors' phones, with one-tap respond.
  • Seven-day plan: water, waste, bins and trips, toilets, Pravasi Mitras.
  • Carrying capacity per site, with the assumptions on screen. v1
  • CCTV people counting, frames blurred; only the count is kept.
  • What visitors say: sentiment and complaint topics per monument.

Honest data. Real: 1,960 CC-licensed monument photos from 202 photographers, ASI annual footfall, OpenStreetMap places and roads, the district tourism book and website, live weather. Simulated and labelled in the app: the hourly crowd shape (calibrated to ASI totals), parking sensor streams, business prices and ratings. Home photos are real photographs relit with AI. Version 1 means working today at a simpler depth — the app says so on the screen, and every pipeline retrains unchanged on real ticket counts and sensors.
